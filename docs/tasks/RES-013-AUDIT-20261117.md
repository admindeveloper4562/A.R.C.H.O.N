## Current implementation inventory

`GitHubSourceAdapter` is read-only and allowlisted. A pinned result retains the
exact repository, full commit SHA, license, bounded file/blob metadata,
optional source date and observation timestamp. It forces
`execution_allowed=False`; command-like fields and execution requests are
blocked, and no clone, subprocess or source-write path is present.

`SourceClaim` and `ResearchFinding` retain source IDs, citations and evidence
references. They describe facts, inferences and uncertainty, but do not carry
permission decisions. The RES-009 implementation persists research records and
preserves source observations, while its audit noted that a durable cross-source
claim graph is still not implemented.

The RES-012 proposal workflow is a bounded, proposal-only control. Research may
submit only `tips` proposals; canonical-path, digest, section, actor and
receipt fields are validated. Independent review must use a different actor,
stale bases are blocked, and the workflow never writes `CODING_HANDBOOK.md`.

`src/jarvis_v2/sda/handoff.py` is a shadow cutover-readiness report. It does not
admit Research proposals, authorize execution, or transfer live ownership.
Search of the Research and SDA packages found no adapter that converts a
source result into an SDA candidate, no Research-owned task admission queue,
and no direct call from Research into the canonical Master submission path.

## Required future bridge contract

An implementation task may add a bounded bridge that:

1. Accepts only a verified `GitHubCommitResult`/`ResearchFinding` and copies
   repository, pinned commit, license, file/blob refs, source date, observation
   time and citations into an immutable proposal.
2. Carries no command, execution, capability or permission request fields.
3. Submits through the deterministic Master candidate interface rather than
   writing SQLite or starting a worker from Research.
4. Requires independent QC and serialized Merger evidence, with owner approval
   for any safety, permission or deployment effect.
5. Rejects duplicate/conflicting proposal IDs and preserves rejected evidence.

This report intentionally does not create that bridge, change task admission,
or claim that a Research finding is executable work.
